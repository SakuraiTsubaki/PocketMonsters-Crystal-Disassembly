# Pokemon Crystal Unown font

The Japanese release `crystal-jp-rev0` is the origin reference. Its 27 uncompressed 2bpp tiles are byte-identical in all 7 verified releases (Japanese, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish), including the official Korean release where available. The two bitplanes are identical, producing a monochrome glyph set.

`graphics/font/unown-font.png` is regenerated directly from the local ROM range. Per-release reports preserve ROM identity, offsets, source and PNG hashes, while no raw ROM bytes are published. Naming is corroborated by `pret/pokecrystal` at `5c877ef55197039c9676e8e3eec6d254b1c40fe8`, `gfx/font.asm`.
