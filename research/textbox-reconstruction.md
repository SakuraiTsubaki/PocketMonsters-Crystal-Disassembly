# Pocket Monsters Crystal Textbox disassembly

`crystal-jp-rev0` is the Japanese origin reference. All 7 cataloged releases (Japanese, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish) verify the box border, repeated-character helper, and expanded palette rectangle. Korean Gold/Silver use a ROM0 farcall wrapper and bank 0x7F orchestration core; their border and palette bodies remain independently hashed. Candidate status is retained and no ROM bytes are published.
